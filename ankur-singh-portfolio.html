About

Connecting exceptional talent with impactful opportunities

As a Strategic AI Talent Partner with 7+ years of global recruitment experience, Ankur specializes in AI-driven technology hiring, talent intelligence, and strategic workforce solutions across the UK & EMEA markets.

Currently with Apexon, he leads end-to-end technology recruitment for enterprise clients within BFSI, healthcare, and digital transformation domains — helping organizations build high-performing teams through data-driven, consultative hiring strategies.

His expertise spans full-cycle recruitment (360°) — talent sourcing, market mapping, stakeholder management, candidate engagement, interview strategy, offer negotiation, and workforce planning across both contract and permanent hiring models.
